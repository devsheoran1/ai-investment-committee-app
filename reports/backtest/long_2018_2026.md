# Long stock backtest 2018-01 → 2026-10 (stress sub-periods)

Same rule-based sleeve as `summary.md` (strategies, ≥3-votes gate, vol-managed sizing, real `plan_orders`, 5 trades/day, stops, next-open fills, zero cost), mean of 2 staggered starts. Return / Sharpe / max drawdown are measured inside each window on the same continuously running fund.

**Survivorship bias:** the universe is today's S&P 500 constituents. Names removed from the index since 2018 (mostly losers, plus bankruptcies and takeovers) are absent and names added after rallying are present, so every long-only number here is flattered, momentum most of all. The stress windows are the least reliable part: the stocks that crashed hardest in 2018Q4/2020/2022 and never came back are missing. Earnings history (PEAD, earnings premium) is yfinance's up-to-100-quarter record; the revisions, quality, insider and news signals are not backtestable.
